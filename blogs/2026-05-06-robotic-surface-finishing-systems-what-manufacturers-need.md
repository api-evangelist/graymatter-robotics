Manufacturers have spent decades automating the processes that lend themselves to robots: welding, assembly, material handling, precision machining. Surface finishing has not followed the same path. Operations like sanding, grinding, polishing, deburring, blasting, and coating preparation require real-time judgment that traditional robots cannot replicate.

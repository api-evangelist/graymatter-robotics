# GrayMatter Robotics

> GrayMatter Robotics is an American industrial AI and robotics company founded in 2020 by SK Gupta, Ariyan Kabir and Brual Shah, headquartered at a 100,000-square-foot AI Robotics Innovation Center in Carson, California. It builds smart robotic cells for surface treatment and finishing in high-mix, high-variability manufacturing — sanding, grinding, polishing, buffing, blasting, spraying, trimming and AI-based dimensional inspection and defect detection — pairing off-the-shelf industrial robot arms, 3D scanners and tools with its own physics-informed AI stack (GMR-AI, marketed as Factory SuperIntelligence / FSI). Its flagship Scan&Sand cell 3D-scans each incoming part and generates the toolpath autonomously, so a cell changes over between parts in minutes with no fixturing and no teach-pendant programming. GrayMatter sells robots-as-a-service on a monthly subscription that bundles hardware, software, training, remote monitoring and 24/7 support, and counts Boeing, Caterpillar and Oshkosh among its customers.
